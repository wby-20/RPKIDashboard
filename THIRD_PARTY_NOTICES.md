# Third-party data notices

## Natural Earth

The collector basemap is derived from **Natural Earth Admin 0 – Countries
(China POV), version 5.1.1**, at 1:10m scale.

Natural Earth raster and vector map data is in the public domain. Attribution
is not required. Suggested credit: **Made with Natural Earth**.

- Dataset: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/blog/admin-0-countries-point-of-views/
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- POV policy: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/disputed-boundaries-policy/

## External measurement data

RPKI, BGP, RIS, NIST, Cloudflare and CAIDA records displayed by the dashboard
remain subject to the terms and citation requirements of their respective
providers. In particular, research use of CAIDA datasets must follow the CAIDA
Acceptable Use Agreement and citation guidance.
